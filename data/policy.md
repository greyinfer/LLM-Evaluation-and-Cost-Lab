# Meridian Home Insurance: Policy Wording (fictional sample)

This is a fictional policy written for testing. Meridian Home Insurance is not a real company.

## Section 1: About this policy
This policy covers the buildings and contents at the insured address shown in your schedule. Cover lasts for 12 months from the start date in your schedule.

## Section 2: Excess
The standard excess is SGD 250 for each claim. For claims caused by escape of water, the excess is SGD 500 for each claim.

## Section 3: What is covered
We cover loss or damage caused by:
- fire, smoke, lightning or explosion
- theft or attempted theft involving forcible and violent entry
- escape of water from fixed pipes, tanks or appliances
- storm, meaning winds above 75 km/h or exceptionally heavy rainfall

## Section 4: Temporary accommodation
If your home cannot be lived in after loss or damage covered by this policy, we pay the reasonable cost of alternative accommodation. The limit is SGD 10,000 for each claim, for no longer than 6 months.

## Section 5: Accidental damage
Accidental damage is an optional add-on. It is only covered if your schedule shows it.

## Section 6: What is not covered
We do not cover:
- wear and tear or gradual deterioration
- damage caused by insects, vermin or other pests
- any loss or damage that happens while your home has been unoccupied for more than 60 days in a row
- theft without forcible and violent entry

## Section 7: Making a claim
Tell us about any claim within 30 days of the incident. For theft, give us a police report within 7 days of discovering the theft. Keep photographs, receipts and repair quotes to support your claim.

## Section 8: Cancellation
You may cancel within 14 days of the start date and receive a full refund, as long as no claim has been made. After 14 days, we refund the premium for the unused period on a pro-rata basis, less an administration fee of SGD 50.

## Section 9: Renewal
We send a renewal notice at least 21 days before your policy ends.

## Section 10: Valuables
The most we pay for any single item of valuables, such as jewellery or watches, is SGD 2,000, unless the item is listed in your schedule.
